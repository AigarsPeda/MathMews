# Rabbit photograph reference, superseded

This historical rabbit study was replaced by a speedometer at the user’s request. The current `Icon clock-fast` Blender scene and runtime icon contain the speedometer.

The earlier Fast icon was rebuilt from visual study of the [side-on running rabbit photograph hosted by UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/human-evolution/sites/human_evolution/files/Leaping_Rabbit.jpg), viewed on 20 October 2026. It appears on UCL's [Human Evolution article](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/human-evolution/news/2017/jul/rabbit-burrowing-casts-doubt-regourdou-homo-neanderthalensis-burial).

The Blender silhouette follows the photographed extended phase of a bound: a long, low torso; small tapered head; narrow upright ears; forelegs reaching forward; bent hocks and long hind feet trailing behind; and a small raised tail. The coat uses natural warm brown with a pale underside, darker ear tips and nose. The reference photograph is linked here, not included in the app.

During this study, the model used the `Icon clock-fast` scene in `docs/art/app-icons/source/icons.blend`, generated by `clock_fast` in `scripts/3d/render_app_icons.py`. The menu continues to use the bundled transparent `assets/icons/clock-fast.png`. Preview: `rabbit-fast-reference.png`.
