# Boss collection and individual endings

THESIS: Completed boss endings become a persistent collection the child can revisit. Each lesson owns an individual boss scene and story.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Math Mews cream pages, white surfaces, peach outlines, mint selection, gold boss accents, system typography and rounded Blender materials. Keep the shipped orange cat and native sheets.

STORY: Solve three saved stages to earn a boss ending. Existing saved completions count automatically. Browse collected or locked bosses, play earned endings freely, and close or replay without changing lives or coins.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home shows Coins, Streak, Achievements with unlocked/total count, Lives, then compact Settings. The achievement count matches the 16-point stat values, with a bold dark unlocked number and a regular muted total. Achievements gets more width, and Lives uses a compact52-point scaled column. The collection has Back, crown-paw icon/title, a count, Collected/All bosses controls and two columns of illustrated boss cards. The earned ending opens in an expanded native sheet with scrollable story and a fixed Close action.

FORM: Extend the incumbent native game interface. No replacement visual world, marketing claims or separate design comp.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, and DESIGN.md
