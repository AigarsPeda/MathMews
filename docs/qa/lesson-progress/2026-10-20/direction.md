STORY: Scan a lesson as Not started, In progress, Boss left, Challenge left, All done or Locked. Read exact progress in three labeled columns. Challenge remains optional and never gates the next lesson or revokes core completion. Enter the lesson through the same row action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep Back, difficulty, topic title and description. Replace the verbose required-progress sentence with a compact Practice + bosses count and visible-track bar. Separate cards show lesson number/status, title/arrow and three illustrated segmented progress groups. Generic per-lesson descriptions and repeated special-level sentences leave the list.

FORM: A refinement of the incumbent native list. On narrow layouts, place activity labels below their icons to preserve words. Use complete localized screen-reader summaries on each row.
