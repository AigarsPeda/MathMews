# Research applied to lesson status

[NN/g Icon Usability](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/icon-usability/) recommends visible labels beside or near unfamiliar icons and simple recognizable artwork. The list therefore keeps Practice, Challenge and Boss labels next to their Blender artwork. On narrow layouts labels move below icons rather than breaking words.

[NN/g Indicators, Validations, and Notifications](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/indicators-validations-notifications/) describes indicators as visual cues attached to content and distinguishes them from notifications. The design applies that distinction by keeping status with each lesson, without an interrupting dialog.

The implementation uses counts, filled/outlined segments, check and lock symbols, and short state labels. Color reinforces these distinctions. Challenge is marked Optional, with its own independent completion indicator, so an unfinished challenge does not imply that the required practice and boss are unfinished.

These references inform the design. They do not constitute a user study of Math Mews. Native verification covers the actual saved states and English/Latvian layouts.
